## Decisions

NEXUS retains Community Model → versioned Recipe → Observation → Evidence →
Attention/Insight → Action → Measurement → Review. No Message Content, DM,
Presence, sentiment, voice recording or cross-server identity analysis is added.

| Tier | Assessment | Launch implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free $0 — Observe | KEEP | Basic multi-surface observation, evidence, coverage, integration health and attention remain valuable and usable. |
| Starter $15 — Understand | KEEP provisionally | History, distributions and comparative Journeys supply a reason to pay. Validate useful diagnosis with real community operators. |
| Growth $49 — Operate & Improve | KEEP provisionally | Requires demonstrated recurring reminders/routing/custom recipes and measured outcomes; usage limits alone are insufficient positioning. |
| Scale $149 — Team & Automate | NEEDS MORE DATA; keep Sandbox amount | Multi-guild, RBAC, API and scheduled reports remain planned. Do not sell them as available. Scale Live stays blocked until its central purchase reasons ship. |

No price is changed. $15/$49/$149 remain INTERNAL / PROVISIONAL monthly USD
Sandbox pricing. No annual NEXUS offer is invented. Starter/Growth/Scale are
testable in Sandbox; Live publication requires independent price, feature,
Discord parity, tax/legal and operational approval. Next validation: five to ten
operator interviews and retained usage through one measurement/review cycle,
then willingness-to-pay and conversion tests with honest available features.
