## Implementation decisions

Free must provide useful Discord-native observation and evidence. Starter must justify $15 through exploration, saved views, 90-day history and aggregate export rather than arbitrary observation restrictions. Growth must complete the Attention → Action → Review loop and supply real read API, outbound webhooks and reports. Scale must supply genuine organization and team governance, not only larger limits. KEEP each provisional price while validating willingness to pay and actual feature readiness. Public sales remain disabled until commercial approval.

MATCH means a comparable implemented outcome; ADAPT means a privacy preserving Operations equivalent; INTEGRATE means an external-provider boundary; EXCLUDE means outside the product or privacy policy. Until verification, this document records decisions rather than availability claims.

## Billing policy evidence

[Discord supported monetization locales](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/17297949965079-How-Do-I-Monetize-My-App) currently list the US, UK and EU. [Required monetization support](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/23810643331735-Premium-Apps-Required-Support-for-Monetizing-Apps) informs the existing applicability and parity policy. Applicability uses trusted operator configuration, never browser locale. A sales restriction must not disable management or reconciliation of an existing contract.

[Stripe pending updates](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/pending-updates), [Billing testing](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/testing) and [Hosted Invoice Page](https://docs.stripe.com/invoicing/hosted-invoice-page) guide upgrade authentication. An unpaid pending update retains the current entitlement; the signed event triggers retrieval of authoritative subscription state after payment. Live Stripe and real-money testing remain disabled.
